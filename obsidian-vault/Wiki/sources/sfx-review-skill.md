# Source: SFX Review Skill

Post-placement review and improvement skill. Reads existing SFX on timeline, analyzes gaps, and recommends delta-only changes (add/remove/move) without full re-placement.

## Key Facts

- **Trigger:** "ใส่น้อย" (too few), "ใส่มากไป" (too many), "ตรวจละเอียด" (detailed check), "เช็คผล" (QC)
- **Delta-only approach:** never re-place everything, only fix what's wrong
- **Impact scoring:** 0-3 scale per subtitle segment, high impact (≥2) + no SFX nearby = candidate for addition
- **Quality metrics:** density, spacing, family variety, reason specificity
- **Reason quality standard:** must include timestamp, specific word/phrase, and why it needs emphasis
