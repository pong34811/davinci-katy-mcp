# Source: Skills Analysis

Definition of skills analysis — evaluating skills and competencies to identify gaps and align capabilities with strategic goals or project needs.

## Key Facts

- **Source:** [trainingindustry.com/glossary/skills-analysis/](https://trainingindustry.com/glossary/skills-analysis/)
- **Published:** 2001-01-31
- **Definition:** Skills analysis is the process of evaluating the skills and competencies of individuals, teams or an organization to determine their strengths, gaps and areas for development.
