## Overview

An AI Skill Assessment is a structured, job-relevant evaluation used in pre-employment testing to measure a candidate’s ability to effectively, responsibly, and productively work with artificial intelligence tools in real workplace scenarios. AI skill assessment focuses on applied performance, critical thinking, and responsible AI usage within the context of real work.
