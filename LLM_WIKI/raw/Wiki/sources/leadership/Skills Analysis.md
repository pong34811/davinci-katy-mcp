# Skills Analysis

> Definition of skills analysis — evaluating skills and competencies to identify gaps and align capabilities.

## Summary

**Skills analysis** is the process of evaluating the skills and competencies of individuals, teams or an organization to determine their strengths, gaps and areas for development. It involves assessing current capabilities against desired or required skills, often to align workforce abilities with strategic goals, job requirements or project needs.
