## FAQs for Skill Gap Analysis

### What is skills gap analysis?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6278a259cc3db4179e7a3a8e/636909ffadefcd768a1256c8_chevron.down.webp)

It’s the process of identifying the gap between the skills your organization needs and what it currently has. This helps leaders prioritize upskilling, hiring, or restructuring to align talent with business goals.

### Why is skills gap analysis important for modern organizations?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6278a259cc3db4179e7a3a8e/636909ffadefcd768a1256c8_chevron.down.webp)

It helps you understand your workforce’s strengths and weaknesses. With that insight, you can make smarter decisions around hiring, training, and promotions—keeping your business agile and competitive.

### How does iMocha help identify skill gaps in an organization?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6278a259cc3db4179e7a3a8e/636909ffadefcd768a1256c8_chevron.down.webp)

iMocha uses AI to compare employee skill data with role-specific benchmarks. This highlights areas for development and pinpoints where your team is ready to grow—or where you may need to hire.
