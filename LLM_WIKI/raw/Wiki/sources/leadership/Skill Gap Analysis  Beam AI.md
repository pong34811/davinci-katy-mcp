## Skill Gap Analysis

Turn role expectations and performance evidence into a review-ready skills-gap analysis.

- ![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/PrlRXInJEOmroUq5TdwGyhUunY.png?width=3620&height=2250)
- ![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/PrlRXInJEOmroUq5TdwGyhUunY.png?width=3620&height=2250)

## What it does

Compares documented role requirements with supplied skills and performance evidence, then identifies possible gaps and development priorities. It supports a structured conversation; it does not provide an objective assessment when the source data is incomplete or biased.

## How it works

Provide current role expectations, relevant skills, observed evidence, career goals, and available learning options. Managers and HR should review privacy, bias, accuracy, accommodations, and employee input before using the analysis in development or performance decisions.

## Example workflows

- Prepare a development discussion for a role transition.
- Compare team capability evidence with planned project needs.
- Prioritize learning topics for an approved training program.
