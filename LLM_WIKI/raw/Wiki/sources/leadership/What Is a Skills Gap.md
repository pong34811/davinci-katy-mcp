## What is a skills gap analysis?

A skills gap analysis is a systematic process organizations use to identify and understand the differences between the skills that their [workforce](https://www.coursera.org/enterprise/articles/digital-talent) currently possesses and the skills needed to achieve organizational goals or complete specific tasks. Organizations assess employees’ existing skills, compare them with the desired skills for their roles, and identify gaps that exist between the two. Furthermore, by identifying this gap, your company can make informed decisions about employee training, hiring practices, and organizational planning.

## What is the importance of conducting a skills gap analysis?

This critical tool empowers you with the information needed to recognize areas where your employees may need additional training or development to perform their jobs effectively. By identifying these gaps, your organization can create targeted [training programs](https://www.coursera.org/enterprise/articles/learning-outcomes), make informed recruitment decisions, and strategically align workforce development with business objectives. Some of the key benefits of performing a skills gap analysis include:

### Identifying employee skills

Analyzing your organization’s current skills makes it clear where the strengths and weaknesses lie. This knowledge can be instrumental in planning future training programs, identifying who needs each training, and developing initiatives.

## How to conduct a skills gap analysis

Your exact process for conducting a skills gap analysis will vary according to the type of company you operate and the resources available. However, skills gap analyses typically include these basic steps:

### 1\. Identify business goals and objectives.

Understanding what your organization aims to achieve is the foundation of a skills gap analysis. Clearly defining your business goals helps align the required skills with your organizational strategy.

### 2\. Determine key skills and competencies.

Identify the [essential skills](https://www.coursera.org/enterprise/articles/essential-managerial-skills) and competencies required to achieve your business objectives. These can include both technical skills, such as using specific software or equipment, and workplace skills, like communication or leadership. You can prioritize these as high- or low-priority skills.

### 3\. Assess current skill levels.

Evaluate the existing skills within your organization. You can do this in several ways, such as surveys, interviews, performance reviews, or direct observation. The aim is to understand your employees’ current capabilities.

### 4\. Identify the gaps.

Compare your employees’ current skill levels with the desired levels. This section essentially functions as the results portion of your analysis. Performing this comparison will help you recognize where the gaps exist and to what degree.

### 5\. Develop a plan.

Create a targeted strategy to address specific gaps and align with your business’s overall objectives. Your plan can include a section on the required skills, actions to develop these skills, and the desired results. If you give feedback individually, this section might offer recommendations or goals to your workforce.
