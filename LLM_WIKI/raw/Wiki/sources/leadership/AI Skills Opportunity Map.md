## AI Increases the Value of Expertise While Making It Harder to Build

Professionals with deep domain expertise are best placed to judge where AI should be applied. Yet pathways for building that expertise are narrowing as AI absorbs entry-level work.

## Management Skill Is Moving Down theHierarchy

Coordination, oversight, and evaluation of AI outputs are becoming foundational skills for early-career professionals, not just senior leaders.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65f1d299b87bcc50550a6367/6a01484740a2bc6a5e353acc_Image%2006.png)

## AI Often Reduces Productivity Before It Improves It

A productivity dip can emerge as AI is integrated into workflows, driven by three mismatches: output volume outpaces human review capacity, AI is applied to the wrong tasks, and task complexity exceeds users’ domain expertise.

## The AI Productivity Paradox: From Early Gains to Sustainable Value

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65f1d299b87bcc50550a6367/6a02b6ebe7dfd3ee16e567dd_Image%2007%20-1.png) ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65f1d299b87bcc50550a6367/6a02b6ecb8b14d4c68873da0_Image%2007%20-2.png) ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65f1d299b87bcc50550a6367/6a02b6ece0fcf5f246973501_Image%2007%20-3.png) ![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/65f1d299b87bcc50550a6367/6a02b3a2751cb1616755a1f8_Image%2007%20-4%20(1).png)

The **three mismatches** that drive the dip.

MISMATCH 01

Output-Review Imbalance

AI rapidly scales the volume and complexity of output, outpacing human capacity to evaluate it.

mismatch 02

AI-Task Misallocation

Without understanding AI tools, people misapply them to tasks, creating friction that accumulates over time.

mismatch 03

Expertise-Complexity Gap

As AI handles more complex work, the expertise needed to evaluate its outputs and processes often exceeds user capability.

## Long-Term AIProductivity DependsMore on Human Capabilities Than on AI Skills

Applied AI skills will continue to evolve, but enduring human capabilities sustain productive and effective AI use over time.
