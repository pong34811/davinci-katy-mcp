###### What Does Skill Gap Mean?

The missing connection between the abilities your employee already possesses and what is genuinely needed to succeed in their role. This shortfall often becomes clear when you notice someone struggling to meet work demands or industry benchmarks. That’s where a skills evaluation helps to spotlight areas that need growth or improvement.

###### What Is an Example of a Skills Gap?

Well, for example, we can think of a marketing specialist who isn’t familiar with tools like Google Analytics or SEO. techniques. Today, these are must-haves for the role. Isn’t this show a clear mismatch between existing abilities and work expectations?

When people don’t keep up with fast-changing tools or knowledge, their abilities suffer. It afterwards affects output, slows growth, and blocks business goals.
