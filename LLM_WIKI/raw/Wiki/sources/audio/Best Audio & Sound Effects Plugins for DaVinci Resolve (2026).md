# Best Audio & Sound Effects Plugins for DaVinci Resolve (2026)

## Three Different Audio Jobs

Audio in DaVinci Resolve is not one problem — the best plugin depends on the job:

1. **Cleaning up noisy dialogue** — [CrumplePop AudioDenoise](https://xere.my/davinci-plugins/crumplepop-audiodenoise/)
2. **Finding royalty-free music** — [Epidemic Sound](https://xere.my/davinci-plugins/epidemic-sound/), [Soundly](https://xere.my/davinci-plugins/soundly/)
3. **Dropping in sound effects** — [Filmkid SFX](https://xere.my/davinci-plugins/filmkid-sfx/), [Advanced SFX Pack](https://xere.my/davinci-plugins/angel-barclay-advanced-sfx-pack/)
