# Higgsfield Plugins for DaVinci Resolve

> AI tools running natively inside DaVinci Resolve — no exports, no switching apps, no manual file handoffs.

## Breakthrough Capabilities

### AI LUT creator
Match any look from a single reference. Drop in a frame, photo, or palette and Higgsfield recolors your footage — transferring tones, contrast, and mood straight into your DaVinci Resolve timeline.

### Generate AI Video
Create any clip with natural language — scenes, characters, B-roll, or camera moves — Higgsfield drops the result straight into your DaVinci Resolve workflow.

### Generate AI Image
Create any visual with natural language — backgrounds, overlays, title cards, scene elements.

### Reframe
Crop any clip to 9:16, 16:9, 4:3, 3:4, 21:9, or 1:1 with one click. Smart subject tracking keeps the focal point centered.

### Remove Background
Pull a clean key from any footage without a green screen. AI detects subjects with pixel-level precision.

### Draw to edit
Sketch directly on the frame to tell Higgsfield what to change — inpainting that works inside DaVinci Resolve.

### Upscale
Boost any clip up to 4K or 8K with real detail recovery, not just pixel stretching.

## Cinema Studio 3.5

A cinematic AI production studio — shape every shot with precise control over camera, lens, and light.

DaVinci Resolve 19 and newer on macOS and Windows. Not compatible with the App Store version — use the version from blackmagicdesign.com.
